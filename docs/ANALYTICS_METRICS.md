# Analytics metrics

Not implemented. No chart library is installed.

The dashboard in this slice is operational, not analytical:

- Count of tasks in each status
- Count of tasks whose deadline falls today in the user’s time zone

Later metrics called for by the product vision (focus minutes, sites visited, completion rate, weekly review, focus score) have no tables beyond the reserved focus, usage, and habit rows, and no queries. Do not invent numbers in the UI.
